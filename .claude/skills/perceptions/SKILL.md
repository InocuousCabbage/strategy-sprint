# Perceptions Exercise (Story Stack)

You are running the Perceptions exercise — defining the key storylines that will drive all content and brand work.

## Why This Exercise Matters

Perceptions are the statements we want our audience to think or say about us. They're our key storylines — the narratives that, if our audience believes them, will drive growth.

Most companies create content reactively — responding to trends, filling a calendar, or copying competitors. Perceptions give content a strategic backbone: every piece of content should ladder up to reinforcing one of these storylines.

When written properly, perceptions:
- Keep content focused on major themes (not scattered topics)
- Give every team member a shared language for "what we're trying to say"
- Make content planning easier (does this piece reinforce a perception?)
- Create compounding brand equity over time

## Step 1: Research

1. **Review prior exercises:**
   - Company Overview: what are our differentiators?
   - ICP Prioritization: what do our audiences care about?
   - Marketing Advantages: what strengths can we credibly claim?

2. **Analyze existing messaging:**
   - What does the website currently say?
   - What themes appear in recent content?
   - What do customers say in reviews/testimonials?

3. **Identify gaps:**
   - What do we want to be known for that we're NOT known for yet?
   - What misconceptions exist about us?

## Step 2: Conversational Discovery

### Current State
1. "If your best customer described you to a peer, what would they say?"
2. "What do people currently believe about your company — both accurate and inaccurate?"
3. "What misconceptions about your company or category frustrate you most?"

### Desired State
4. "In 12 months, what do you want your target audience to believe about you?"
5. "What's the one thing, if every prospect believed it, would make selling effortless?"
6. "Are there industry shifts or trends you want to own the narrative on?"

### Differentiation Narratives
7. "What can you credibly claim that no competitor can?"
8. "What's the contrarian or non-obvious take your company has about the industry?"
9. "What story from a customer best demonstrates your value?"

### Content Alignment
10. "What topics does your team have genuine expertise in?"
11. "What content has resonated most with your audience so far?"
12. "Are there content themes you've been meaning to pursue but haven't?"

## Step 4: Client Confirmation

Present the perceptions:

- "Is the priority ranking right? Which perception is most urgent?"
- "Are the proof points credible? Anything we should add or remove?"
- "Do the content pillars feel actionable?"

Make corrections based on feedback.
